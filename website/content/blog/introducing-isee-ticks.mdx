Engineers already have great tools for watching fast-moving systems: metrics, rules, alerts and dashboards. Markets are
fast-moving systems too. **iSee Ticks** applies that toolbox to NSE equities, indices and derivatives.

## See every tick

A streaming exporter subscribes to the broker's WebSocket feed and publishes every instrument as Prometheus metrics: last
price, day change, volume, circuit limits and five levels of market depth. Order-book imbalance and other derived KPIs are
computed right next to the raw data.

## Observe every move

Recording rules turn raw metrics into a catalogue of market KPIs: breadth, sector moves, pivot and reference levels,
derivatives signals and risk measures. Each KPI has a contract and a test, so a dashboard number always means the same
thing.

Alertmanager sends price, circuit, flow and portfolio alerts, and it knows the NSE calendar: no pages at 2 a.m. or on
exchange holidays.

## One home for all of it

You should not need PromQL to answer "what moved and why". The product home puts market overview, watchlist, movers,
sectors, a stock explorer and alerts in one fast UI, and links to Grafana whenever you want to dig deeper.

## Try it

The [live demo](/demo) runs the whole stack on one small cloud VM, fed by the built-in market simulator. It is read-only,
needs no sign-in, and every number in it is synthetic.
